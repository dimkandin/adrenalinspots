Lake Baikal — Maloe More (Kurkut Bay / Olkhon)

Irkutsk Oblast, Olkhonsky District, Russia · Kitesurfing

Season: January–March, June–September.

The Maloye More (“Small Sea”) is the strait between Baikal's western shore and Olkhon Island, with shallow, warm bays (Kurkut, Mukhor) where you can ride flat water in summer. In winter the wind blows the snow away and the ice of the Maloye More becomes a vast playground for ice- and snowkiting (best in February–March, when the ice is over 70 cm thick). A place for experienced riders: the wind is strong and very gusty.
