Mavrovo (Zare Lazarevski)

Mavrovo National Park, western North Macedonia, North Macedonia · Skiing and snowboarding

Best season: January–February.

North Macedonia's main ski resort, on the shore of Lake Mavrovo in a national park: about 15 km of pistes (other sources say up to 27 km), ~620 m vertical, with ski tours to Mount Korab (2764 m) nearby. Very cheap and quiet, but the lifts are mostly old T-bars. The resort is run by Jahorina's operator (OC Jahorina). A higher alternative is Popova Šapka near Tetovo (1672–2380 m, ~18 km of pistes, 1,300 MKD).
