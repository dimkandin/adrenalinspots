Tarifa — Valdevaqueros / Los Lances

Andalusia, Costa de la Luz (Strait of Gibraltar), Spain · Kitesurfing

Best season: April–September.

Tarifa is the southernmost point of continental Europe and Spain's main kite resort: the Venturi effect in the Strait of Gibraltar accelerates the easterly Levante and the westerly Poniente. The main spots are the 4-km Valdevaqueros beach (with the dune and Punta Paloma) and the long Los Lances by the town. Dozens of schools, rentals and shops, and a lively scene.
