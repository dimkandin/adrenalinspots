Annecy — Col de la Forclaz

Lake Annecy, Haute-Savoie, France · Paragliding

Best season: April–September.

The Col de la Forclaz above the eastern shore of Lake Annecy is one of the most visited paragliding sites in the world. The takeoff looks out over the turquoise lake and the Alps, and the landing field is by the southern shore in Doussard. There are dozens of schools and tandem companies, and pilots get reliable thermals and XC routes into the Pre-Alps.
